Fonts

Drop TTF / OTF / WOFF anywhere to add one. A Sketch SVG's text finds its font by family, PostScript name or file name ("Inter-Bold").

Paper & roll

Align, from any state of the roll: feed until a label's leading (top) edge is out past the tear bar, measure how far it sticks out, and enter it. A short or partial label works too: only its edge counts.

Screen scale

At 1:1 the paper shows at its real size. To set this screen: 1:1, hold a real ruler to the ruler over the paper with its 0 at the screen's 0, and read it at the screen's mark.

Click something on the page.

Drag to move (0.5 mm steps, Alt: free), the square corner to scale (Shift: stretch), the round knob to turn (15° steps, Shift: free).
